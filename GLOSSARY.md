# Waypoint

A personal coach app: you name something you want in life, and Waypoint interviews you, charts a route to it, and walks it with you day by day, bending the route when life gets in the way.

## Language

**Aspiration**:
The life-level outcome the user wants, in their own words ("become more fit", "start my own company").
_Avoid_: Goal, objective, dream

**Short name**:
A label of at most 16 characters for an Aspiration ("Half marathon", "Spanish"), proposed by the Coach at the end of the Interview and editable, used where the full words don't fit.
_Avoid_: Nickname, tag, title

**Interview**:
The conversation in which the coach gathers the full picture of an Aspiration: where the user stands, constraints, motivation, and what success looks like.
_Avoid_: Onboarding, questionnaire, survey

**Coach**:
The intelligence that interviews, plans, re-plans and encourages the user.
_Avoid_: Bot, assistant, AI

**Plan**:
The route the Coach charts from where the user is now to an Aspiration, made of Milestones and Actions.
_Avoid_: Roadmap, program, schedule

**Milestone**:
A dated checkpoint on a Plan that shows real progress toward the Aspiration ("company is live by March"), with a plain statement of when it counts as reached: work done or an ability gained, something the user can make true by doing the planned work, never an outcome they can't fully control.
_Avoid_: Goal, target, sub-goal

**Measure**:
An optional number next to a Milestone that shows whether the work is moving the outcome ("waist, now 96 cm, hoping for about 92"); a test of whether the Plan is working, never a condition for reaching the Milestone.
_Avoid_: Metric, KPI, check, score

**Action**:
A concrete thing to do on a given day, either recurring (a habit) or one-off (a task).
_Avoid_: Goal, todo, step

**Guide**:
The Coach's short how-to for an Action: what to do and how, fitted to the user ("three recipes and a shopping list" for a meal prep, "the exercises, sets and rest" for a strength session).
_Avoid_: Instructions, tips, content, notes

**Hoped-for date**:
When the user hopes to reach an Aspiration; the Plan's Milestones are dated toward it.
_Avoid_: Deadline, target date

**Settle-for date**:
The latest an Aspiration can be reached and still feel worth it to the user; the line a Re-plan tries not to cross.
_Avoid_: Deadline, fallback date

**Settle-for picture**:
A smaller version of the Aspiration that would still feel worth it to the user ("clearly leaner and stronger"); held alongside, or instead of, a Settle-for date.
_Avoid_: Minimum goal, fallback, plan B

**Time budget**:
The hours a week the user gives to all their active Aspirations together, split into a share for each.
_Avoid_: Capacity, availability, allowance

**Slot**:
A recurring time the user is usually free ("weekday mornings 07:00–07:45"), where the Coach places Actions.
_Avoid_: Time block, window, availability

**Anchor**:
A moment in the user's existing routine ("after dinner, around 19:30", "weekday dinners") that a habit is tied to instead of a Slot.
_Avoid_: Cue, trigger, routine

**Check-in**:
The user's report on an Action: done, partly done, or skipped.
_Avoid_: Log, completion, tick

**Pace**:
How much of its planned time the user has actually been giving an Aspiration lately, measured against the Plan as it stood each week.
_Avoid_: Velocity, speed, performance, score

**Projection**:
The Coach's honest estimate of when the work planned for a Milestone will be done if the current Pace holds. It speaks of effort done, never of the outcome itself.
_Avoid_: Forecast, prediction, ETA

**Weekly refresh**:
The Coach's proposal, on a day and time the user picks, for the coming week of every Aspiration: unreported Actions to confirm, what's planned, and what changed and why. It applies itself after a day if the user doesn't answer.
_Avoid_: Weekly review, sprint, reset

**Re-plan**:
The Coach's adjustment of a Plan after a Check-in or a message from the user, without blame.
_Avoid_: Reschedule, penalty, reset

**Contact frequency**:
How often the user is happy for the Coach to reach out unprompted, shared across all their active Aspirations; reminders for their own Actions don't count against it.
_Avoid_: Notification level, cadence, nudge limit

**Pause**:
A stretch of time set aside for one or all of the user's Aspirations (the rest of today, a few days, or open-ended after a long silence), during which the Coach stays quiet and the Actions in it count neither as done nor as skipped. The user starts it, or the Coach does after two weeks of silence.
_Avoid_: Break, day off, snooze, vacation mode

**Catch-up**:
Extra Actions spread over the weeks after missed Actions or a Pause, to recover lost progress so Milestones can keep their dates.
_Avoid_: Make-up, penalty, overtime
