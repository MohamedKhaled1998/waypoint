## Weekly refresh

**Here's what I changed**

- **Strength session:** I made one step up, a little more weight on goblet squats, because they felt easy and you did all 3 sessions. Everything else stays the same so the ramp stays gentle.
- **Strength session:** I swapped the bench press for push-ups, since that's what you've been doing. If the last set gets hard, put your hands on the bench to make it easier. Don't add reps there yet.
- **Meal prep:** The chicken tray is now split into two flavours, so Thursday and Friday don't taste like Monday. Bean chili can still be one of your boxes if you liked it.
- **Meal prep:** I cut the time to about 75 minutes by running everything in parallel: one tray, one pot, eggs on the side.
