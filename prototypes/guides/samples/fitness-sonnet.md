## Weekly refresh

**Here's what I changed**

- **Meal prep, flavors:** each box now has one of two seasonings (chili-garlic-soy, or cumin, paprika, lemon and chili) plus a different topping each day, so Thursday isn't the same as Monday.
- **Meal prep, time:** it's now one tray and one pot, aiming for about 60-75 minutes. Your chili took nearly two hours, and a Sunday that runs long makes the habit harder to keep. Your bean chili is a fine backup on a week you want a change.
- **Strength, goblet squat:** you did all three sessions and the squats felt easy, so the goblet squat goes up by one set, to 3. That's the only step up this week.
- **Strength, push-ups:** no increase, since they were hard by the last set. They stay in the warm-up, and you can put your hands on the bench to make them easier.
- **Everything else:** unchanged.
